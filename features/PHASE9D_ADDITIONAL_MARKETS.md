## Canada: Health Canada Medical Device License

### Regulatory Pathway

**Classification:** Medical Device — Class III (highest risk class for diagnostic devices)

**Authority:** Health Canada Therapeutic Products Directorate (TPD) or Biologic and Radiopharmaceutical Drugs Directorate (BRDD)

**Timeline:** 4-6 months standard review

### Requirements Beyond FDA 510(k)

```
Core Documentation (same as FDA):
✅ Device description
✅ Performance data
✅ Safety & effectiveness data
✅ Risk management file

Canada-Specific Requirements:
□ License application (Form MDEL 0020)
□ Biocompatibility testing (if applicable — N/A for software)
□ Clinical evaluation report (reference FDA + new Canadian evidence)
□ Manufacturing quality system (ISO 13485 certification)
□ Post-market surveillance plan (specific to Canadian requirements)
□ Labeling in English AND French
□ Canadian contact person (licensed professional in Canada)
□ Chemistry/Manufacturing/Controls (CMC) — N/A for pure software
```

### Key Differences from FDA

1. **Earlier Clinical Evidence Required**
   - FDA: Can submit with predicate equivalence + performance data
   - Health Canada: Requires more comprehensive clinical evidence
   - **Mitigation:** Use FDA approval as evidence of safety/effectiveness

2. **ISO 13485 Certification**
   - FDA: QSR compliance sufficient
   - Health Canada: Requires ISO 13485:2016 certification
   - **Timeline:** 3-4 months for ISO audit and certification

3. **Labeling in French**
   - All device labeling, instructions for use, marketing claims must be in French
   - **Effort:** 2-3 weeks translation + regulatory verification

### Submission Process

**Phase 1: Pre-Submission Meeting (Week 1-2)**
- Contact Health Canada TPD
- Present product overview
- Get feedback on regulatory pathway
- Discuss any special requirements

**Phase 2: Dossier Preparation (Week 3-8)**
- Compile all required documentation
- Ensure ISO 13485 certification
- Translate labeling to French
- Prepare clinical evaluation report
- Risk management file

**Phase 3: Submission (Week 8)**
- Submit MDEL application with supporting dossier
- Health Canada reviews for completeness
- Initial Assessment (2-4 weeks)

**Phase 4: Health Canada Review (Week 12-20)**
- Standard review (90 days)
- May request additional information
- Respond to information requests (30-day SLA)

**Phase 5: Approval & Issuance**
- Health Canada issues Medical Device License
- Device can be marketed in Canada
- Post-market reporting obligations begin

### Cost & Timeline
- **Cost:** $50-80K (regulatory consultant, ISO 13485 audit, translation)
- **Timeline:** 6 months (4-6 months review + 2 months prep)
- **Go-Live:** January 2027

## Australia: TGA Therapeutic Goods Administration

### Regulatory Pathway

**Classification:** Class IIb or III (in vitro diagnostic device — moderate to high risk)

**Authority:** TGA (Australian medicines and medical devices regulator)

**Timeline:** 3-4 months standard track

### Requirements Beyond FDA

```
Core Documentation (same as FDA):
✅ Device description
✅ Performance data
✅ Risk management

Australia-Specific:
□ Australian Register application
□ TGA conformity assessment (demonstrates compliance with standards)
□ IEC 62304:2015 compliance documentation
□ Clinical evidence (can reference FDA approval)
□ Quality system (ISO 13485 certification required)
□ Labeling in English (Australia & New Zealand)
□ Australian Sponsor (local representative required)
□ Post-market surveillance plan
```

### Key Differences from FDA/Canada

1. **Stricter Software Requirements**
   - TGA requires explicit cybersecurity documentation
   - Must demonstrate software doesn't introduce new risks
   - Required: Software security, data integrity, audit trail validation

2. **IEC 62304:2015 Mandatory**
   - Explicit cybersecurity requirements (new in 2015 version)
   - Risk-based software development lifecycle
   - Configuration management plan required

3. **Australian Sponsor Requirement**
   - Must have local Australian registered business
   - Can be distributor/partner, but must be involved in post-market surveillance
   - **Mitigation:** Partner with Australian distributor/reseller

### Submission Process

**Phase 1: Pre-Submission (Week 1)**
- Contact TGA
- Provide product summary
- Get guidance on classification & requirements

**Phase 2: Establish Australian Sponsor (Week 2-4)**
- Identify/establish local Australian company
- Register as TGA sponsor
- Establish post-market surveillance arrangement

**Phase 3: Conformity Assessment (Week 5-10)**
- Document device history record
- Risk management summary
- Software lifecycle documentation
- Cybersecurity assessment
- Clinical evaluation report

**Phase 4: TGA Application (Week 10)**
- Submit application through TGA online system
- Includes all conformity assessment documentation
- Australian Sponsor signs declaration

**Phase 5: TGA Review (Week 10-18)**
- Standard assessment track (8 weeks)
- May request clarification
- Response time: 10 working days

**Phase 6: Approval & Registration**
- Device enters Australian Register
- Can be marketed immediately
- Annual reporting requirements

### Cost & Timeline
- **Cost:** $40-60K (regulatory consultant, Australian sponsor setup, TGA fees)
- **Timeline:** 4-5 months
- **Go-Live:** December 2026

## Japan: PMDA Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency

### Regulatory Pathway

**Classification:** Medical Device Class III or IV (highest risk classes for diagnostics)

**Authority:** PMDA (equivalent to FDA in Japan)

**Timeline:** 8-12 months (longest of all markets)

### Requirements Beyond FDA

```
Core Documentation (same as FDA):
✅ Device description
✅ Performance data
✅ Risk management

Japan-Specific:
□ PMDA pre-submission meeting (mandatory)
□ Clinical data package (more extensive than FDA)
□ Japanese language documentation (all labeling, IFU, summaries)
□ Quality Overall Summary in Japanese
□ Nonclinical summary in Japanese
□ Clinical summary in Japanese
□ GCP compliance for any new clinical studies
□ ISO 13485:2016 certification
□ Japanese Authorized Representative (local legal entity required)
□ Comprehensive post-market surveillance plan
□ Device master record (all manufacturing specifications)
```

### Key Differences from US/EU/Australia

1. **Most Stringent Clinical Requirements**
   - Japan requires extensive clinical evidence
   - FDA approval alone insufficient
   - May require Japan-specific clinical study

2. **Extensive Documentation Requirements**
   - All technical/clinical documents in Japanese
   - Translations certified by professional translators
   - More detailed quality system documentation

3. **PMDA Advisory Process**
   - Pre-submission meeting with PMDA (mandatory)
   - PMDA provides specific guidance on what's needed
   - Can iterate on approach before formal submission
   - Adds 2-3 months to timeline

4. **Japanese Authorized Representative**
   - Must be registered with PMDA
   - Responsible for regulatory compliance
   - Can be subsidiary, distributor, or third-party consultant
   - Adds complexity & cost

### Submission Process

**Phase 1: PMDA Pre-Submission Meeting (Month 1-2)**
- Request meeting with PMDA
- Present product, intended use, and regulatory strategy
- PMDA provides written guidance on requirements
- Discuss clinical evidence, quality system, software

**Phase 2: Clinical Evidence Package (Month 3-6)**
- Compile FDA approval + clinical data
- Determine if new Japan-specific clinical study needed
- Commission certified Japanese translations
- Prepare clinical evaluation report

**Phase 3: Quality System Documentation (Month 6-8)**
- Document manufacturing specifications (not needed for cloud software)
- ISO 13485 certification (required for company, not just device)
- Device history records and traceability
- Software lifecycle & cybersecurity documentation

**Phase 4: Regulatory Dossier Assembly (Month 8)**
- Combine all Japanese language documentation
- Quality Overall Summary
- Nonclinical Summary
- Clinical Summary
- Safety Specification & Post-Market Plan

**Phase 5: PMDA Formal Submission (Month 9)**
- Submit via PMDA online system
- PMDA performs completeness check (2-4 weeks)
- Formal review begins

**Phase 6: PMDA Review & Questions (Month 9-18)**
- Standard review track (9 months)
- PMDA may issue Information Requests (IR) cycles
- Each IR: 60-day response deadline
- May have 1-3 IR cycles

**Phase 7: Approval & Licensing**
- PMDA issues approval notification
- Device registration in Japan
- Can launch 30 days after approval notification

### Cost & Timeline
- **Cost:** $150-250K (regulatory consultant, clinical evaluation, translations, certified translators, authorized representative setup)
- **Timeline:** 12-14 months (most time-consuming market)
- **Go-Live:** August 2027

## Post-Approval Operations

### Multi-Country Adverse Event Reporting

```
Adverse Event Detected (Any Country)
  ↓
Report to all regulatory authorities:
  - US: FDA MedWatch (15 days)
  - Canada: Health Canada MDRN (30 days)
  - Australia: TGA (at earliest convenience)
  - Japan: PMDA (30 days)
  - EU: Notified Body (10 days)
  ↓
Unified internal investigation
  ↓
Corrective action plan
  ↓
Follow-up reports to all authorities
```

### Annual Reporting

- **US:** Annual Medical Device Reporting (MDR) summary
- **Canada:** Annual Summary Report to Health Canada
- **Australia:** Annual report to TGA
- **Japan:** Periodic safety reports to PMDA
- **EU:** Periodic Safety Update Report (PSUR) to Notified Body
