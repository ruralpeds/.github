33-Week RDS — CPAP Setup and Surfactant Decision

Clinical Explanation
INSURE: FiO₂ > 0.30 on CPAP ≥ 5–6 cmH₂O → Intubate-SURfactant-Extubate. Poractant alfa 200 mg/kg preferred over 100 mg/kg (Ramanathan 2004 RCT). SpO₂ 91–95% for all preterm infants.
Decision Points
Decision Point 1
✓ Correct Action:
INSURE threshold met (FiO₂ > 0.30 on CPAP ≥ 5 cmH₂O). Intubate briefly, give surfactant, extubate to CPAP. Poractant alfa 200 mg/kg × 1.92 kg = 384 mg ET (4.8 mL). Increase CPAP to 6–7 cmH₂O post-surfactant. Target SpO₂ 91–95%.
⚠ Wrong Action Consequence:
Continuing CPAP without surfactant when FiO₂ > 0.30 on adequate pressure allows ongoing lung injury.
Decision Point 2
✓ Correct Action:
Target SpO₂ 91–95% for 33-week infant. AVOID SpO₂ > 95% (ROP, BPD). AVOID < 90% (mortality — BOOST-II). Alarms: low 89%, high 96%. Recheck for CPAP failure: FiO₂ creep > 0.30, increasing WOB.
⚠ Wrong Action Consequence:
SpO₂ 95–100% in preterm = oxygen toxicity → ROP, BPD.
Key Teaching Points
  • INSURE threshold: FiO₂ > 0.30 on CPAP ≥ 5 cmH₂O → surfactant
  • Poractant alfa preferred dose: 200 mg/kg
  • Preterm SpO₂ target: 91–95%
  • CPAP failure: FiO₂ > 0.40, increasing WOB, apnea
  • Second surfactant: if FiO₂ returns > 0.30 after 6–12h
References
  • Sweet DG et al. European RDS Guidelines 2023. Neonatology 2023;120:3.
  • BOOST-II. Lancet 2013;382:514.
Learning Objectives
  • Apply initial CPAP settings for 33-week RDS
  • Identify INSURE threshold: FiO₂ > 0.30 on CPAP ≥ 5 cmH₂O
  • Calculate surfactant dose: 200 mg/kg
  • Apply SpO₂ targets 91–95%
