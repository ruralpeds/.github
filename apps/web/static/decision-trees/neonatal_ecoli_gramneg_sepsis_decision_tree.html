Key Reminders for Rural Settings

  • Antibiotics within 60 minutes of recognizing sepsis
  • Blood culture minimum volume: 1 mL
  • Ampicillin resistance in E. coli: 70–85% — gentamicin is the critical drug
  • Do NOT routinely substitute cefotaxime for gentamicin
  • E. coli meningitis = 21 days minimum treatment
  • Serial physical exams by experienced nurses are the most sensitive screening tool
  • If cultures negative at 24–36 hours and infant improving: STOP antibiotics
