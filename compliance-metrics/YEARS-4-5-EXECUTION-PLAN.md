## RISKS & MITIGATION (YEARS 4-5)

### Market Adoption Risk (20% probability)
- **Risk:** Slower than projected deployment pace; market education challenges
- **Impact:** Lower Year 4-5 revenue; extended break-even timeline
- **Mitigation:** Strong clinical evidence from pilots; targeted partnerships; focused market education
- **Contingency:** Adjust expansion pace; focus on high-performing early sites; extended ROI timeline (Year 5-6 rather than Year 4-5)

### Competitive Threat (15% probability)
- **Risk:** Competitor device cleared or superior product emerges
- **Impact:** Market share pressure; pricing pressure
- **Mitigation:** FDA clearance regulatory moat; continuous product improvement (v2.0); strong clinician relationships
- **Contingency:** Accelerate v2.0 release; explore adjacent indications; evaluate partnerships

### Regulatory Changes (5% probability)
- **Risk:** FDA additional requirements; pediatric compliance evolving
- **Impact:** Potential design modifications; additional submissions
- **Mitigation:** Proactive FDA engagement; post-market surveillance; design change procedures established
- **Contingency:** Submit design change notifications; adapt to regulatory evolution

### Team Retention (10% probability)
- **Risk:** Key personnel departures; organizational instability
- **Impact:** Deployment delays; knowledge loss
- **Mitigation:** Competitive compensation (aligned with valuation growth); career development opportunities; strong team culture
- **Contingency:** Cross-training; outsourced support; interim staffing solutions
