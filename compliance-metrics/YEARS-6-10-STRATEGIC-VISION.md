## RISK ASSESSMENT (YEARS 6-10)

### Key Risks & Mitigation

**Risk 1: Competitive Entry (20% probability)**
- **Scenario:** Competitor develops comparable solution; seeks FDA clearance
- **Timeline:** Year 7-9 (3-4 year development cycle)
- **Mitigation:** Maintain clinical thought leadership; continuous product improvement; build network effects
- **Contingency:** Accelerate product expansion (additional indications); pursue strategic partnership/acquisition

**Risk 2: Market Adoption Slower Than Projected (15% probability)**
- **Scenario:** Health systems slower to adopt; deployment pace declines
- **Impact:** Revenue $1.5M (vs. $2M+) by Year 10
- **Mitigation:** Strengthen value proposition; partnership with health system networks; targeted marketing
- **Contingency:** Shift to acquisition/partnership path; accept lower acquisition valuation

**Risk 3: Regulatory Changes (10% probability)**
- **Scenario:** FDA strengthens Class II device requirements; additional post-market surveillance
- **Impact:** Increased compliance costs; timeline extensions
- **Mitigation:** Proactive FDA engagement; anticipate regulatory evolution; build compliance capabilities
- **Contingency:** Design change submission; adapt to new requirements

**Risk 4: M&A Integration Challenges (If Acquisition Path - 15% probability)**
- **Scenario:** Acquirer integration issues; cultural misalignment; talent loss
- **Impact:** Performance decline; earnout not achieved; limited synergy realization
- **Mitigation:** Structure earnout carefully; retain key talent; establish integration playbook
- **Contingency:** Carve-out or spinoff strategy; pursue independent growth path
