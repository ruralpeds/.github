## RISK MITIGATION & CONTINGENCY PLANNING

### Critical Risks & Mitigation Strategies

**Risk 1: FDA Clearance Delay Beyond Q3 2027**
- Probability: 15% (due to deficiency requiring additional response)
- Impact: 3-4 month clinical deployment delay
- Mitigation: Robust pre-submission meeting (Q4 2026) reduces deficiency probability; contingency response readiness prepared
- Contingency: Defer Year 4 expansion 1-2 quarters; adjust revenue timeline
- Financial impact: $30-50K additional counsel costs

**Risk 2: Pilot Site Clinical Efficacy Not Meeting Expectations**
- Probability: 5% (very unlikely given rigorous validation completed pre-submission)
- Impact: Design modifications required; clinical validation expansion
- Mitigation: Robust validation testing (Q3 2026) ensures clinical performance; pilot site selection emphasizes appropriate use cases
- Contingency: Design improvements (2-3 months), re-validation (2-3 months), supplemental FDA submission
- Financial impact: $50-100K additional engineering + counsel costs; 6-month revenue delay

**Risk 3: Regulatory Environment Change (e.g., additional requirements)**
- Probability: 5% (low; healthcare regulatory environment relatively stable)
- Impact: Compliance modifications; potential timeline extension
- Mitigation: Engagement with FDA post-clearance; proactive regulatory intelligence; industry associations
- Contingency: Regulatory counsel escalation; design modification submission (510(k) or Design Change)
- Financial impact: $20-40K additional counsel costs; potential 2-3 month timeline impact

**Risk 4: Market Adoption Slower Than Projected**
- Probability: 20% (market education and adoption cycles unpredictable)
- Impact: Year 4-5 deployment targets not achieved
- Mitigation: Strong clinical evidence from pilots; targeted partnership approach; operational efficiency gains
- Contingency: Adjust expansion pace; focus on high-performing initial sites; extended ROI timeline (Year 5-6 rather than Year 4-5)
- Financial impact: Reduced revenue (but profitable operations continue); delayed strategic outcomes

**Risk 5: Competitive Threat (similar device cleared or superior product emerges)**
- Probability: 15% (healthcare software market competitive; but regulatory pathway creates barrier)
- Impact: Market share pressure; pricing pressure
- Mitigation: FDA clearance provides regulatory moat; continuous product improvement; strong clinician relationships; network effects
- Contingency: Accelerate product improvements; expand to adjacent indications; explore partnerships/acquisitions
- Financial impact: Reduced margins or slower growth; long-term strategic value maintained
