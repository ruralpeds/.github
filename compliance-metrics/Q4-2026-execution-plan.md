### Week 3–4 (Nov 15–30): FDA Pre-Submission Meeting

**FDA Pre-Submission Meeting (Scheduled Nov 15–20):**
- Location: FDA's Office of Product Evaluation (virtual or in-person)
- Attendees: Compliance Officer, CTO (technical lead), FDA regulatory counsel
- Duration: 2–3 hours

**Pre-Submission Topics:**
1. **510(k) Scope & Predicate Device**
   - Device intended use (neonatal growth assessment, clinical decision support)
   - Predicate device selection (CLSI pediatric growth analyzer, Class II)
   - Substantial equivalence argument (design, performance, safety)

2. **Performance Testing Approach**
   - Algorithm validation (reference standard comparison)
   - Accuracy testing (sensitivity, specificity, positive/negative predictive values)
   - Use error testing (clinician interpretation, UI usability)
   - Environmental testing (software stability under various conditions)

3. **Regulatory Pathway Confirmation**
   - 510(k) versus PMA assessment
   - Submission timeline feasibility
   - Expected review period (90 days standard)

4. **QMS & Post-Market Compliance**
   - ISO 13485 implementation status
   - Post-market surveillance plan
   - Adverse event tracking procedures
   - Design change submission process

**Expected FDA Feedback:**
- ✅ 510(k) pathway confirmed (most likely outcome)
- ✅ Predicate device accepted
- ✅ Performance testing scope validated or refined
- Any deficiency areas flagged for remediation (target: minimal)

**Post-Meeting Actions (Nov 21–30):**
- [ ] Regulatory counsel summarizes FDA feedback
- [ ] Internal team reviews feedback
- [ ] 510(k) package scope adjusted if needed
