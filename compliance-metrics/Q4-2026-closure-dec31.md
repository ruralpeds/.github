## LESSONS LEARNED & BEST PRACTICES

**Successes:**
1. **FDA Pre-Submission Meeting Value:** Early alignment with FDA prevented scope creep and validated pathway
2. **Counsel Integration:** External regulatory counsel reduced internal burden and ensured compliance
3. **QMS-First Approach:** Implementing QMS during package development (not after) ensured seamless integration
4. **Team Ownership:** Cross-functional team engagement (Engineering + Compliance + IT) ensured quality outcomes
5. **On-Budget Execution:** Careful planning and resource allocation kept spending at 99.9% of budget (minimal waste)

**Recommendations for Future Submissions:**
1. Schedule FDA pre-submission meeting earlier (October would have provided more feedback integration time)
2. Establish counsel engagement in advance (negotiation delays can compress schedules)
3. Conduct QMS software setup in parallel with package development (sequential approach used here was efficient, but parallel would save 2 weeks)
4. Plan for counsel review iterations (built in 5-day buffer; could be reduced to 3 days)
5. Schedule FDA auditor earlier (audit in Nov-Dec would have provided earlier compliance signals)
