## Major Quality Frameworks and Organizations

### Vermont Oxford Network (VON)
- Largest neonatal quality collaborative (>1,200 NICUs)
- Standardized data definitions for VLBW outcomes
- Benchmarking against network-wide performance
- iNICQ (Internet-based Neonatal Improvement Collaborative for Quality)

### California Perinatal Quality Care Collaborative (CPQCC)
- State-level quality metrics
- Perinatal equity initiatives
- Transport quality indicators

### Joint Commission / CMS
- Perinatal Core Measures (PC-01 through PC-06)
- PC-01: Elective delivery before 39 weeks
- PC-02: Cesarean section rate
- PC-05: Exclusive breast milk feeding
- PC-06: Unexpected complications in term newborns

### AAP / ACOG
- Levels of Neonatal Care guidelines (quality implications)
- Perinatal quality indicators

## Categories of Quality Metrics

### Process Metrics
- Golden Hour compliance rates
- Time to first antibiotic for suspected sepsis
- Exclusive human milk feeding rates
- Hypothermia prevention (admission temperature)
- CCHD screening completion
- Antenatal steroid completion rates
- Delayed cord clamping rates
- Skin-to-skin within 1 hour rates

### Outcome Metrics
- Mortality (overall, risk-adjusted)
- BPD rates at 36 weeks PMA
- NEC rates (Bell Stage ≥2)
- Severe IVH (Grade III/IV) rates
- ROP requiring treatment
- Late-onset sepsis / CLABSI rates
- Length of stay
- Growth velocity during NICU stay
- Unplanned extubation rates
- Pneumothorax rates

### Balancing Metrics
- Antibiotic utilization rates (antibiotic stewardship)
- Blood culture utilization
- Mother-infant separation rates
- Readmission within 30 days

### Patient/Family Experience
- Family-centered care metrics
- Parent satisfaction
- Breastfeeding support measures
- Discharge preparedness

## Measurement Methodologies

### Statistical Process Control (SPC)
- Control charts (p-charts, u-charts, X-bar charts)
- Run charts for rapid-cycle improvement
- Special cause vs. common cause variation identification

### Risk Adjustment
- CRIB-II (Clinical Risk Index for Babies)
- SNAP-II (Score for Neonatal Acute Physiology)
- Illness severity adjustment for outcome comparisons
- Case-mix adjustment for benchmarking

### Benchmarking Approaches
- Internal trending (facility over time)
- External comparison (VON network, CPQCC)
- Funnel plots for institutional comparison
- Risk-adjusted comparison models

### Quality Improvement Methodologies Applied
- Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycles
- Model for Improvement (IHI)
- Lean / Six Sigma in NICU settings
- Potentially Better Practices (PBPs) — VON model
- Collaborative improvement networks
