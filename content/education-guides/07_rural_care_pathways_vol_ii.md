## Overview

This volume extends the care pathway framework established in Volume I to additional clinical domains specific to rural Level II nursery practice. It addresses the unique challenges of providing intermediate neonatal care in resource-limited settings with particular attention to transfer decision-making, telemedicine integration, and staffing considerations.
