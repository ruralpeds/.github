## Overview

The most widely adopted clinical protocols for critically ill and convalescing newborns share a common evidence base — primarily AAP guidelines, VON quality benchmarks, and AWHONN/NANN nursing standards — yet meaningful institutional variation persists in thresholds, timing, and implementation.

**Institutions surveyed**: CHOP, Brigham & Women's/Boston Children's, Johns Hopkins All Children's, Texas Children's, UC Davis, UCSF, Cincinnati Children's, Nationwide Children's, University of Iowa, and others.

## Selected Comparative Data

### Feeding Advancement Rates by Weight

| Weight | CHOP | BWH/Boston | Johns Hopkins |
|--------|------|-----------|---------------|
| ≤1000g | 20 mL/kg/day after 3-day trophic | 10 mL/kg/day q12h | Up to 30 mL/kg/day |
| 1001–1500g | 30 mL/kg/day after 1–2-day trophic | 15 mL/kg/day q12h | 30 mL/kg/day |
| >1500g | Per clinical need | 15–20 mL/kg/day q12h | 30 mL/kg/day |

Target full enteral volumes: **150–160 mL/kg/day** across institutions. Fortification with HMF begins at **60 mL/kg/day** across CHOP, BWH, UC Davis, and UCSF. Texas Children's feeds 100% human breast milk to all NICU infants <1500g, reducing NEC rates from national average 10–12% to **2%** since 2009.

### Growth Targets

- Adequate weight gain: **>18 g/kg/day** (BWH); <15 g/kg/day × 7 days = growth faltering
- Length: >0.8 cm/week
- Head circumference: 0.5–1 cm/week
- Enteral protein: **3.4–4.5 g/kg/day**; energy 110–150 kcal/kg/day

### Hypoglycemia Management

All institutions follow AAP time-based thresholds. Screening begins 30 minutes after first feed. Persistent hypoglycemia defined as inability to maintain glucose >50 mg/dL in first 48 hours or >60 mg/dL thereafter.

### Emerging Practices Identified

- **Eat Sleep Console** for NAS (replacing Finnegan scoring at many centers)
- **LISA** (Less Invasive Surfactant Administration) gaining adoption
- **Aflibercept** for ROP treatment (emerging alongside bevacizumab)
- **Cue-based feeding** programs (e.g., Nationwide Children's SIMPLE program reducing LOS by ~15 days)
