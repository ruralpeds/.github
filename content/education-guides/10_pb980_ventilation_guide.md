## PB 980 Neonatal Capabilities Overview

### Available Modes

**Breath types**: Volume Control (VC), Pressure Control (PC), Volume Control Plus (VC+ / PRVC), Pressure Support (PS), Volume Support (VS)

**Ventilation modes**: A/C, SIMV, SPONT, BiLevel, CPAP — each combinable with breath types yielding: A/C-VC, A/C-PC, A/C-VC+, SIMV-VC+PS, SIMV-PC+PS, SPONT-PS, SPONT-VS, BiLevel-PC+PS

**Non-invasive modes**: NIV-CPAP, NIV-A/C PC (synchronized NIPPV), NIV-SIMV PC+PS. NIV+ software calibrates to specific neonatal interfaces.

### Modes NOT Available
- **NAVA**: Proprietary to Getinge/Maquet — not available on PB 980
- **HFV (HFOV/HFJV)**: Not available; requires SensorMedics 3100A or Bunnell LifePulse
- **PAV+ for neonates**: Minimum 3.5 kg predicted body weight — unsuitable for ELBW/VLBW

### Monitoring
- Real-time waveforms: pressure-time, flow-time, volume-time
- Loops: pressure-volume, flow-volume
- Calculated values: CDYN, RDYN, C20/C ratio, RSBI, peak/mean/plateau pressures, spontaneous/mandatory rates, minute volume, I:E ratio, percent leak
- Apnea detection: 10–60 seconds interval with backup ventilation (available even in NIV-CPAP)
- Optional neonatal capnography

## Non-Invasive Ventilation on PB 980

### CPAP Settings
| Population | Starting CPAP | Post-extubation ELBW |
|-----------|--------------|---------------------|
| 23–32 weeks | 5–6 cmH₂O | 7–9 cmH₂O (up to 11) |
| Late preterm | 4–6 cmH₂O | — |
| Term | 4–5 cmH₂O | — |

Escalation: FiO₂ >0.30–0.40 on CPAP ≥6 → surfactant (LISA/INSURE). Max CPAP generally ≤8 cmH₂O (pneumothorax risk per COIN trial).

### NIPPV via NIV-A/C PC
PIP 14–25 cmH₂O, PEEP 5–6, Rate 20–40, Ti 0.3–0.5 sec. Leak Sync ensures reliable flow triggering.

### BiLevel
High pressure 8–12, low pressure 4–6 cmH₂O, 10–30 cycles/min. Niche role in TTN (7.9% vs 20% treatment failure vs CPAP in ≥34 week trial).

## Invasive Ventilation Strategies

### Initial Settings by Population

| Population | Mode | PIP/VT | PEEP | Rate | Ti | FiO₂ |
|-----------|------|--------|------|------|----|------|
| 34–36 wk RDS | A/C-PC or VC+ | 18–22 / 4–6 mL/kg | 5–6 | 30–40 | 0.35–0.4 | Titrate to SpO₂ |
| Term RDS | A/C-PC or VC+ | 20–25 / 5–7 mL/kg | 5 | 25–40 | Titrate | Titrate |
| MAS | SIMV-PC+PS | 25–30 / 5–8 mL/kg | 5–6 | 40–60 | 0.4–0.5 | Often high |
| PPHN | A/C-PC | 20–28 / 5–7 mL/kg | 5 | 40–60 | 0.3–0.4 | 1.0 initially |

### Troubleshooting — DOPE Mnemonic

For acute desaturation: **disconnect from ventilator → hand-bag with 100% FiO₂**

- **D**isplacement: Check ETT depth, auscultate bilaterally
- **O**bstruction: Attempt to pass suction catheter; if unable → reintubate
- **P**neumothorax: Transilluminate (preterm), assess for unilateral absent breath sounds + hypotension; needle decompress if tension
- **E**quipment: Verify gas supply (35–87 psi), circuit integrity, humidifier function

### ETT Management

| Weight | GA | ETT (mm ID) | Depth at lip (cm) | Suction catheter (Fr) |
|--------|-----|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| <1 kg | <28 wk | 2.5 | 5.5–6.5 | 5–6 |
| 1–2 kg | 28–34 wk | 3.0 | 7.0–7.5 | 6 |
| 2–3 kg | 34–38 wk | 3.5 | 8.0–8.5 | 6–8 |
| >3 kg | >38 wk | 3.5–4.0 | 9.0 | 8 |

Suction pressure: 60–80 mmHg (max 100–120 for tenacious secretions), ≤5–10 seconds, closed suction systems preferred.

### VAP Prevention Bundle
- Hand hygiene; HOB elevation 15–30°
- Oral care q3–4h with expressed breast milk or sterile water (no chlorhexidine in neonates)
- Daily extubation readiness assessment
- Circuit changes q7–14 days
- Closed suctioning; sterile intubation technique
- Post-bundle VAP rates: 0.5–2.5 per 1,000 ventilator days (from 8–11 pre-bundle)

### Weaning and Extubation

**Extubation readiness** (9-criteria scoring system in original):
- Stable on low ventilator settings (PIP ≤14–16, PEEP 5, Rate ≤15–20, FiO₂ <0.30)
- Spontaneous breathing effort
- Adequate blood gases
- Caffeine on board (for <34 weeks)
