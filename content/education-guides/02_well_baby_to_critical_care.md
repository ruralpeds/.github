## Part III: Critically Ill Newborn Assessment (Selected Content)

### Respiratory Distress Severity — Silverman-Anderson Score

| Feature | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---------|---|---|---|
| Chest movement | Synchronized | Lag on inspiration | See-saw |
| Intercostal retractions | None | Just visible | Marked |
| Xiphoid retractions | None | Just visible | Marked |
| Nasal flaring | None | Minimal | Marked |
| Expiratory grunt | None | Audible with stethoscope | Audible without stethoscope |

**Score interpretation**: 0 = no distress; 1–3 = mild; 4–6 = moderate; 7–10 = severe (consider intubation)

### Hypoglycemia Management — AAP Time-Based Thresholds

| Time Period | Action Level | Intervention |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| Birth to 4 hours | <25 mg/dL | IV D10W 2 mL/kg bolus + infusion |
| Birth to 4 hours | 25–40 mg/dL | Feed + recheck in 1 hour |
| 4–24 hours | <35 mg/dL | IV D10W 2 mL/kg bolus + infusion |
| 4–24 hours | 35–45 mg/dL | Feed + recheck |
| >48 hours | <50 mg/dL | Evaluate + treat |

**GIR Formula**: GIR (mg/kg/min) = [Rate (mL/hr) × Dextrose %] ÷ [Weight (kg) × 6]

### Physician Notification Criteria

| Parameter | Notify Immediately | Urgent Assessment |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Heart Rate | <80 or >200 bpm | <100 or >180 bpm |
| Respiratory Rate | >80 or apnea >20 sec | >60 persistent |
| Temperature | <35.5°C or >38.0°C | <36.0°C or >37.5°C |
| SpO₂ | <85% | <90% on RA or requiring FiO₂ >0.40 |
| Blood Glucose | <25 mg/dL (0–4h); <35 (4–24h) | Persistent 25–45 range |
| Blood Pressure (MAP) | <GA in weeks | Decreasing trend |
| Color | Central cyanosis, mottling, pallor | Persistent acrocyanosis >24h |
| Neurologic | Seizure activity, bulging fontanelle | Lethargy, hypotonia, irritability |

### SBAR Communication Framework — Neonatal Examples

**Situation**: "I'm calling about Baby Smith, a 35-week male, now 6 hours old. I'm concerned about increasing respiratory distress."

**Background**: "Born via SVD to a 28-year-old G2P2, maternal GBS positive with adequate IAP. Apgars 7 and 9. Birth weight 2,450g. Initial transition was unremarkable."

**Assessment**: "Current vitals: HR 168, RR 72, SpO₂ 91% on room air, temp 36.8°C. Silverman-Anderson score is 5 — moderate retractions, nasal flaring, audible grunt. Breath sounds are diminished bilaterally with fine crackles."

**Recommendation**: "I think this baby needs supplemental oxygen and a chest X-ray. I'd like orders for oxygen to maintain SpO₂ 90–95%, continuous monitoring, and your assessment at bedside."

**CUS Escalation Words**:
- **C** — "I am **Concerned**"
- **U** — "I am **Uncomfortable**"
- **S** — "This is a **Safety** issue"

## Part V: Practical Protocols (Selected Content)

### IV Fluid Management

**Standard maintenance fluids for neonates ≥34 weeks**:
- Day of life 1: 60–80 mL/kg/day D10W
- Day of life 2: 80–100 mL/kg/day
- Day of life 3+: 100–120 mL/kg/day (advance to 150 mL/kg/day as tolerated)

**Target GIR**: 4–6 mg/kg/min initially; adjust based on glucose monitoring
