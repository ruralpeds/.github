## Gap Analysis

No single, freely available document exists that combines real-time clinical documentation with embedded clinical decision support for all neonatal emergencies in rural/Level I nurseries. Seven categories of published resources were identified, all USA-based:

1. **S.T.A.B.L.E. Program** (7th edition, AAP): 910,000+ providers trained; PSSAT form is QI-focused, not a medical-record document
2. **NRP Algorithm** (8th/9th edition): Gold standard for delivery room resuscitation; ends at post-resuscitation handoff
3. **Children's Minnesota Neonatal Stabilization Guide**: 36-page pocket booklet, richest single clinical reference
4. **State perinatal quality collaboratives**: CPQCC/CPeTS transport form (TRIPS score), FPQC Golden Hour Guidelines, ILPQC (70% reduction in moderate/severe hypothermia)
5. **NALS program** (2025, American Red Cross/AWHONN): Targets maternity care deserts
6. **Published academic literature**: CMAT analysis found best neonatal cognitive aid achieved only 53% adherence to design principles
7. **No validated comprehensive emergency documentation flowsheet** for Level I/CAH settings exists in indexed literature

## Seven-Section Flowsheet Design

### Section 1 — NRP Resuscitation Record
- Time-stamped documentation of resuscitation steps
- Pre-populated SpO₂ targets by minute of life
- Medication dosing: Epinephrine 0.01–0.03 mg/kg IV (1:10,000) or 0.05–0.1 mg/kg ETT

### Section 2 — Immediate Post-Resuscitation Assessment
- Apgar scoring grid
- Cord gas documentation
- Initial vital signs

### Section 3 — S.T.A.B.L.E. Stabilization Grid
Four time-point columns (Baseline, 30-min, 60-min, Transport arrival) organized by:

**S — Sugar**: Blood glucose, IV access, fluid type/rate, GIR calculation, dextrose gel documentation
- Alert: <25 mg/dL (0–4h) or <35 (4–24h) → D10W 2 mL/kg IV bolus STAT
- GIR formula: [Rate (mL/hr) × Dextrose %] ÷ [Weight (kg) × 6]

**T — Temperature**: Axillary temp, warming method, room temp
- Target: 36.5–37.5°C; if HIE suspected and ≥36 wk → initiate passive cooling (33–34°C rectal)

**A — Airway**: RR, WOB, SpO₂, FiO₂, respiratory support, breath sounds
- Embedded ETT reference:

| Weight | GA | ETT (mm) | Depth at lip (cm) | Blade |
|--------|-----|----------|-------------------|-------|
| <1 kg | <28 wk | 2.5 | 5.5–6.5 | 0 |
| 1–2 kg | 28–34 wk | 3.0 | 7.0–7.5 | 0 |
| 2–3 kg | 34–38 wk | 3.5 | 8.0–8.5 | 1 |
| >3 kg | >38 wk | 3.5–4.0 | 9.0 | 1 |

Quick rule: Depth (cm) = Weight (kg) + 6

**B — Blood Pressure**: HR, BP, cap refill, pulses, pre/post-ductal SpO₂
- MAP target ≈ GA in weeks (first 72 hours)
- Differential >3% → suspect PDA, PPHN, or ductal-dependent lesion → consider PGE1

**L — Lab Work**: Glucose, CBC with differential, I:T ratio, blood culture, blood gas, CRP, bilirubin, blood type/Coombs
- Sepsis workup triggers: maternal fever, ROM >18h, GBS+/unknown, chorioamnionitis, preterm labor, respiratory distress, temperature instability
- Give antibiotics within 1 hour of decision to treat

**E — Emotional Support**: Parent communication, visitation, pastoral/social work, lactation support

### Section 4 — Condition-Specific Checklists (11 conditions)
Flip-card format, one condition per card:
- 4A: Prematurity
- 4B: Respiratory distress (RDS, TTN, MAS, pneumothorax)
- 4C: Sepsis
- 4D: HIE / Birth asphyxia
- 4E: Congenital heart disease
- 4F: Surgical emergencies (gastroschisis, omphalocele, CDH, TEF)
- 4G: Seizures
- 4H: Severe hyperbilirubinemia
- 4I: Hydrops fetalis
- 4J: NAS / NOWS
- 4K: Pneumothorax (needle decompression protocol)

### Section 5 — Weight-Based Emergency Dosing Table (0.5–4.0 kg)

Pre-calculated for 9 weight categories including:
- Epinephrine (IV and ETT)
- Normal saline bolus (10 mL/kg)
- D10W bolus (2 mL/kg)
- Ampicillin (100 mg/kg)
- Gentamicin (4–5 mg/kg)
- Surfactant
- PGE1 (with mixing instructions)
- UVC depth formula: [(weight kg × 3) + 9] / 2 + 1

### Section 6 — Ongoing Monitoring Grid
15-minute interval documentation: HR, RR, SpO₂, FiO₂, BP, Temp, Glucose, Resp support, Interventions, Initials
- Alarm values printed as footer

### Section 7 — SBAR Transport Handoff Form

Structured format with:
- **S**: Baby/mother identification, reason for transport, current status
- **B**: Maternal history (G/P, complications, GBS, ROM, delivery, resuscitation, Apgars, cord gases)
- **A**: Current vitals, respiratory status, cardiovascular, neurologic, glucose, IV access, labs
- **R**: Medications given with times, procedures performed, X-rays, blood products, current concerns, parent contact, consent documentation, items sent with baby

## Physical Format Recommendations

- **Sections 1–3**: Laminated card stock, write-on/wipe-off with dry erase marker
- **Section 4**: Laminated flip cards on ring binder at resuscitation station
- **Section 5**: Single laminated landscape page mounted at warmer
- **Section 7**: Two-sided consumable paper form (one copy retained by referring facility)
