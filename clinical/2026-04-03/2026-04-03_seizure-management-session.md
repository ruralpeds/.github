## Summary

Comprehensive clinical education package created for rural ED and Level I/II nursery providers covering acute seizure management, chronic epilepsy sick day management, breakthrough seizure evaluation, and neonatal seizure protocols. All content grounded in PubMed-sourced evidence including the ESETT trial (NEJM 2019), EcLiPSE and ConSEPT trials (Lancet 2019), ILAE 2023 neonatal seizure guidelines, and multiple systematic reviews and network meta-analyses. Expanded drug profiles for levetiracetam (Keppra) and fosphenytoin were added based on targeted PubMed searches.
