## Summary

Created a comprehensive three-output clinical education package on Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CAH) acute illness management and sick day glucocorticoid dosing. Research was conducted via PubMed and web search, grounding all content in the Endocrine Society 2018 Clinical Practice Guideline (Speiser et al.), the NIH longitudinal study (El-Maouche et al. 2018), the I-CAH international survey (Ali et al. 2022), BSPED 2023 consensus guidance, and additional peer-reviewed sources.
